# SOR Team Board: Privacy Policy

_The published text lives in the website (`PRIVACY` in index.html, served at https://team.systemoverload.org/privacy). Keep this copy in step. Last updated 2026-11-25._

The SOR Team Board app and website (team.systemoverload.org) are run by FRC Team 6059 System Overload Robotics for its own students, mentors and parents. They're used to organize the team's tasks, meetings, signups and attendance. They have no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share your information for marketing.

## What we collect

- **Your Google account basics:** your name, email address and profile photo. These come from Google when you sign in.
- **What you tell us when you join:** whether you're a student, lead, Board member, mentor or parent, and whether you identify as female. Only you and team leads can see that last answer. The team uses it to make sure every in-person meeting has the adults it needs.
- **Your team work:** tasks, checklists and comments you write or are assigned, and photos you add to tasks (deleted after 14 days unless the team keeps them).
- **Meetings:** your meeting signups, attendance marks, check-ins, and "I'm here" notices.
- **Slack:** if you link your Slack account, its member ID, so the team's Slack bot can match your reactions and messages to your account.
- **The helper:** if you use the AI helper (on the website, or in the app after you allow it), your questions and the board details it needs are sent to Google's Gemini service, and every chat is saved so the team can stop misuse of the team's key.

The app doesn't collect your location, contacts, health information or advertising identifiers.

## Who can see it

Approved team members can see names, photos, tasks, comments and the meeting calendar. Leads and the Board can also see attendance and the answers above, so they can run the team. Parents can see only what parents need.

## Where it's stored

- **Google Firebase:** sign-in and the team's database.
- **The team's server:** runs the website and the team's Slack bot.
- **Slack:** for linked accounts and for meeting signups made in Slack.
- **Google Gemini:** for the website's helper only.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy terms.

## Students under 18

Most of our members are high-school students. We collect only what the team needs to run, and only approved team members can see it. A parent or guardian can ask us to show, correct or delete their student's information at any time.

## Deleting your account

In the app, go to **You › Delete my account**. This deletes your profile, your private answers and your sign-in. Records that belong to the team stay: tasks and comments you wrote, and attendance history. You can also ask any Board member or lead mentor, in person or on the team's Slack, to delete your account and information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and post the date at the top.
